then review and import the resulting source through the existing V2 workflow.

admission cadence/cap and generated regeneration. It does not activate a writer.

requirements. A conflicting exact member becomes a separate source subject;
its dependents park while independent members continue. Focused conclusive red

outcomes and reach a named publication gate: the shared default-branch writer
still requires trusted exact-head green. No mode manufactures CI evidence.

Their filing, leases, stop proof, preservation and cleanup use shared ports and
the existing keen-stone-14 recovery. Only a named human gate can hold forever.
