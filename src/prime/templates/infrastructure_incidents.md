### Infrastructure incidents

Shared workspace preparation failures are infrastructure incidents. Correlate
the session path, claim file, Git worktree/branch, database locks and retained
integration owner or job pins. Preserve bounded retries and retained
preparations, and escalate one incident with the affected paths and evidence.
Never mass-pause otherwise ready tasks to quiet preparation failures. Existing
human holds remain binding. Resume only after the infrastructure repair is
proved safe; never release another live session's workspace or branch.
