Long-form specimen

The reading face is the one decision that is hard to walk back. A marketing page can survive a merely competent body face because nobody reads more than forty words of it; a blog post cannot. Newsreader was drawn for reading news on screens, carries real optical sizing, and holds its color at the reading size the--text-reading token sets — which is why the measure here is capped at--measure-prose rather than allowed to run the width of the container.

Long lines are the other half of that decision. A line the reader's eye has to hunt back across costs more than any font choice gains, so the measure is a token and not a per-page judgement call.
